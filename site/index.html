I live in Colorado. I studied Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, with an embedded systems emphasis and a minor in Philosophy focused on information ethics.

Army, Qualcomm and FIRST

I served in the Colorado Army National Guard from 2017 to 2023. I started as a wheeled vehicle mechanic (91B), then moved into logistics and interpreting. From 2021 to 2022 I deployed to the Middle East with a maintenance company supporting HIMARS. I led a 35-soldier platoon and earned the Army Commendation Medal for hands-on repairs and system reprogramming.

Since 2026 I have been Lead Robotics / Physical AI Staff Software Engineer, Developer Relations at Qualcomm. Robotics and physical AI developer relations is a function that started because of my work. Today it means deploying VLA models on edge NPUs and building with the Arduino UNO Q. In 2025 I moved to AI and DevRel engineering as a Staff Software Engineer. Before that I was a Senior Embedded Software Engineer on 5G modem firmware, after internships in 2020 and in 2019 on the Android Display team.

In FIRST I have been a technical mentor for FRC Team 2972 at the Dawson School in Lafayette, CO since 2023, and an FTC Field Technical Advisor in Colorado since 2022. I have judged since 2017, including Dean's List judging, and I was a Control System Advisor from 2015 to 2018.
