Cats at the door

Miso’s café

Scoring

  • +2for each pair of touching cats with the same coat
  • −3for each pair of touching rivals: Ginger and Smoke, Tuxedo and Snow
  • +5for each row filled from wall to wall
How to play

Each round, four cats wait at the door. You and your rival each take one — you pick first on odd rounds, your rival on even ones — and seat it somewhere on your own floor. Cats can be rotated and flipped, but never moved once seated.

Tap a cat to pick it up (tap it again to rotate), tap or drag on the floor to choose a spot, then seat it. With a mouse, just click the spot. A cat that fits nowhere has to be sent away.

After twelve rounds, the café with the most points wins.

Keyboard: 1–4 pick a cat, arrows move it, R rotates, F flips, Enter seats it.

Who’s running the café next door?

Pick a rival. You’ll share the cats at the door.
