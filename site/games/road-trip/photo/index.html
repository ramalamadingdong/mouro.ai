Where are we going?

You see a photo of a spot on the road ahead. Guess how many minutes until you drive past it; the closer the guess, the more points.

Demo drive plays a trip from your From and To (or Boulder to Colorado Springs if they’re empty) at high speed, with no GPS, so you can try it at home.

Your location goes to OpenStreetMap, to find the route, and to Wikimedia Commons, to find photos near it. It is never sent to mouro.ai, never shared with other players, and not saved on this phone. Past games keep only the date and score. Separately, mouro.ai counts visits with cookie-free analytics: the page, the kind of device, and a rough area worked out from your internet address, never your GPS position.
