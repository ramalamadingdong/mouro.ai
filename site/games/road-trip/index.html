Where are we going?

Two games for the passenger seat. Town trivia: each time you cross into a new town or county, a question about it, then a fact. Photo guess: a photo of a spot on the road ahead. Guess how many minutes until you pass it.

Demo drive plays a trip from your From and To (or Boulder to Colorado Springs if they’re empty) at high speed, with no GPS, so you can try it at home.

Categories

A word game for everyone in the car. It reads out a letter and a category (“C, things in a kitchen”), and you take turns naming one before the timer runs out. Stall and you’re out; the last one talking wins the round. No GPS needed.

Car screen: one phone sits in a mount on the console, shows each turn big and reads it out, with a few big buttons anyone can tap. Passengers’ phones can join with its code as remotes. The room carries the word game only, never anyone’s location, and only phones with the code can see it.
